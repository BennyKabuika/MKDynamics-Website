# Surface brief: marketing site (all routes)

Scope: full site redesign, every route under app/. Visitor mode: Persuade (home, services, about), Read (service detail, opportunity detail, FAQ), Operate (contact and apply forms).

Audience: mixed; Belgian SMEs, startups and independents, organisations. Secondary: internship applicants.
Job: understand what MKDynamics does, trust it, then write or call. Applicants: read a role, then apply.
Proof on hand: 50+ projects claim, security specialism, Ternat address, phone, email, office hours. No portfolio, no testimonials.
Constraints: keep routes, English copy, Next 13 static export.
