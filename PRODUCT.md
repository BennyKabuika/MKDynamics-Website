# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

A mix of prospective clients with no single priority segment: Belgian SMEs looking for a local IT partner, startups and independents launching a product, and organisations (churches, non-profits, schools). Secondary audience: students, self-taught developers, designers and career changers looking for internships.

## Product Purpose

Marketing site for MKDynamics, an IT services company based in Ternat, Belgium. It explains the services, builds trust, and turns visitors into contact requests (contact form, phone, email). A second path collects internship applications (CV upload).

## Positioning

One partner for the whole digital chain: creative design, web and mobile development, and security know-how together, rather than separate agencies for each.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from search or referral, often on mobile. Success is a message through /contact or an application through /resumecv. Contact forms post to Next.js API routes that send email via nodemailer.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services: Website Development, Mobile App Development, Graphic Designing, Security Consultancy, Automation.
- Opportunities: Creative (design) Intern, Developer Intern, Security Intern, each with Available/Unavailable status.
- Routes to preserve: /, /about, /servicess (+ 5 sub-pages), /Opportunity (+ 3 detail pages), /faq, /contact, /resumecv. URLs are indexed (sitemap), so they stay unchanged.
- Stack: Next.js 13 App Router, Tailwind 3, framer-motion. Copy stays in English.

## Product Principles

1. Clarity over hype: a visitor understands what MKDynamics does within one screen.
2. Every page leads to one action: talk to us, or apply.
3. Only true claims; the site proves competence through its own craft.
4. Security is part of the identity, not a separate upsell.
